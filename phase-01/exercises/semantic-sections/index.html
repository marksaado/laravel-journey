How to make a good espresso

To make a good espresso, force near-boiling water through finely ground coffee under high pressure (around 9 bars) using a proper espresso machine

What you need

  • Espresso Machine
  • Espresso Burr Grinder
  • Fresh Whole Bean Coffee

Steps

Preheat your machine, grind 18g of fresh beans finely, distribute and tamp the grounds level into your portafilter, and brew for 25-30 seconds until you yield 36g of espresso.
